# Take Action Against AI Bias and Discrimination

## Immediate Actions You Can Take

This document provides concrete steps that individuals, organizations, and communities can take right now to fight AI bias and discrimination, particularly against Black people.

## For Individuals

### 1. Educate Yourself and Others

**This Week:**
- [ ] Watch the ["Coded Bias" documentary](https://www.codedbias.com/)
- [ ] Read at least one article from ProPublica's "Machine Bias" series
- [ ] Share information about AI bias on your social media

**This Month:**
- [ ] Read one book on algorithmic bias (see RESOURCES.md for recommendations)
- [ ] Follow organizations fighting AI bias on social media
- [ ] Have conversations with friends and family about AI discrimination

**Ongoing:**
- [ ] Stay informed about AI bias cases and research
- [ ] Share educational content regularly
- [ ] Correct misinformation when you encounter it

### 2. Support Organizations Fighting AI Bias

**Financial Support:**
- Donate to organizations like:
  - Algorithmic Justice League
  - Data for Black Lives
  - Color of Change
  - AI Now Institute
  - ACLU (for their work on surveillance and AI)

**Non-Financial Support:**
- Follow and amplify their work on social media
- Sign petitions and support campaigns
- Volunteer your skills if applicable
- Attend their events and webinars

### 3. Advocate for Change

**Contact Your Representatives:**
- Support the Algorithmic Accountability Act
- Advocate for AI regulation and oversight
- Support bans on biased facial recognition systems
- Push for transparency in government AI use

**Local Advocacy:**
- Attend city council meetings about surveillance technology
- Support local organizations fighting discriminatory tech
- Participate in community discussions about AI

**Workplace Advocacy:**
- If your company uses AI, ask about bias audits
- Advocate for diverse hiring in tech roles
- Support ethical AI policies in your organization
- Raise concerns about potentially biased systems

### 4. Know Your Rights

**If You're Affected by AI Discrimination:**

1. **Document Everything**
   - Keep detailed records of discriminatory treatment
   - Save screenshots, emails, and other evidence
   - Note dates, times, and specific incidents
   - Document any human interactions related to AI decisions

2. **File Complaints**
   - EEOC: For employment discrimination
   - FTC: For unfair business practices
   - State consumer protection agencies
   - Relevant regulatory bodies for specific industries

3. **Seek Legal Help**
   - Contact civil rights organizations
   - Consult with attorneys specializing in discrimination
   - Reach out to organizations like ACLU for support
   - Consider joining class action lawsuits if applicable

4. **Share Your Story**
   - Contact investigative journalists (e.g., ProPublica, The Markup)
   - Reach out to advocacy organizations
   - Share on social media (if comfortable)
   - Help raise awareness to prevent others from being harmed

## For Organizations and Companies

### 1. Conduct Bias Audits

**Initial Assessment:**
- [ ] Inventory all AI/ML systems in use
- [ ] Identify high-risk applications
- [ ] Assess current testing and monitoring practices
- [ ] Document known issues and limitations

**Regular Auditing:**
- [ ] Implement quarterly bias audits
- [ ] Test systems across demographic groups
- [ ] Monitor production systems for bias
- [ ] Document and share audit results

**Third-Party Audits:**
- [ ] Hire independent auditors for critical systems
- [ ] Engage with civil rights organizations
- [ ] Participate in industry auditing standards
- [ ] Publish audit results publicly (when appropriate)

### 2. Improve Data Practices

**Data Collection:**
- [ ] Ensure diverse and representative data

- [ ] Use diverse teams for data labeling

**Data Documentation:**
- [ ] Create datasheets for every dataset

- [ ] Track data provenance
- [ ] Update documentation regularly

### 3. Build Diverse Teams

**Hiring:**
- [ ] Commit to diverse hiring in AI/ML roles
- [ ] Remove bias from hiring algorithms
- [ ] Partner with organizations supporting underrepresented groups in tech
- [ ] Create inclusive job descriptions

**Retention and Growth:**
- [ ] Create inclusive work environments
- [ ] Provide growth opportunities for all employees
- [ ] Address pay equity
- [ ] Support employee resource groups

**Advisory Boards:**
- [ ] Establish diverse AI ethics advisory boards
- [ ] Include community representatives
- [ ] Empower boards to influence decisions
- [ ] Act on advisory board recommendations

### 4. Implement Ethical AI Policies

**Policy Development:**
- [ ] Adopt ethical AI principles
- [ ] Create clear guidelines for AI development
- [ ] Establish review processes for high-risk AI
- [ ] Define accountability structures

**Example Policy Elements:**
```markdown
Our Ethical AI Principles:

1. Fairness: We commit to building fair AI systems that don't 
   discriminate based on protected characteristics.

2. Transparency: We provide clear information about how our AI 
   systems work and their limitations.

3. Accountability: We take responsibility for the impact of our 
   AI systems and provide recourse for those harmed.

4. Privacy: We respect user privacy and implement strong data 
   protection measures.

5. Human Oversight: We maintain meaningful human oversight of 
   automated decisions, especially in high-stakes contexts.

Implementation:
- All AI systems undergo fairness review before deployment
- Regular audits of deployed systems
- Clear processes for contesting automated decisions
- Transparency reports published annually
- Dedicated team for AI ethics and fairness
```

### 5. Enable Contestability and Recourse

**Provide Clear Processes:**
- [ ] Allow individuals to contest automated decisions
- [ ] Provide explanations for AI decisions
- [ ] Implement human review for high-stakes decisions
- [ ] Create clear escalation paths

**Track and Learn:**
- [ ] Monitor contested decisions for bias patterns
- [ ] Analyze complaints and feedback
- [ ] Iterate and improve based on learnings
- [ ] Report on issues and improvements
