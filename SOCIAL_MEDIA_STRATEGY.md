## Executive Summary

**Primary Goals:**
- Build brand awareness in cognitive enhancement and wellness space

## 3. Brand Voice & Guidelines

### 3.1 Brand Personality

**Core Traits:**
- **Knowledgeable:** We know our science and research
- **Approachable:** We explain complex topics simply
- **Empowering:** We inspire action and growth
- **Authentic:** We're real, honest, and transparent
- **Calming:** Our tone reflects our mindfulness values
- **Progressive:** We embrace innovation and new ideas

### 3.2 Tone Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use inclusive language (we, us, together)
- Be encouraging and supportive
- Share evidence and research
- Acknowledge challenges and struggles
- Use emojis sparingly and purposefully
- Ask questions to encourage engagement

**Don't:**
- Make medical claims or diagnoses
- Use jargon without explanation
- Be preachy or condescending
- Create anxiety or fear
- Over-promise results
- Engage in controversial debates

### 3.3 Visual Identity

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: Deep cosmic blue (#1A1F4C)
- Secondary: Stellar purple (#6B4DB8)
- Accent: Bright cyan (#00D4FF)
- Neutrals: White, light gray, dark gray

**Typography:**
- Headlines: Modern sans-serif (e.g., Montserrat, Poppins)
- Body: Clean, readable sans-serif (e.g., Open Sans, Inter)

**Imagery Style:**
- Calming, minimalist aesthetics
- Cosmic/stellar themes
- Authentic people (not overly stock-photo)
- Clean compositions with breathing room
- Consistent filter/editing style

**Logo Usage:**
- Always maintain clear space
- Use on contrasting backgrounds
- Never distort or modify
- Include in all branded content

## 9. Crisis Management

### 9.1 Potential Crisis Scenarios

- Negative product reviews going viral
- Customer service failures
- Data breach or privacy concerns
- Controversial statements from team members
- Product defects or issues
- Misleading claims or misunderstandings

### 9.2 Crisis Response Protocol

**Level 1 - Minor Issues (Negative comments):**
- Response time: Within 1 hour
- Action: Acknowledge, apologize, provide solution
- Escalation: Community manager handles

**Level 2 - Moderate Issues (Multiple complaints):**
- Response time: Within 30 minutes
- Action: Pause scheduled posts, craft response, escalate
- Escalation: Social media manager + customer support lead

**Level 3 - Major Crisis (Viral negative content):**
- Response time: Immediate
- Action: Emergency meeting, halt all social activity, prepare statement
- Escalation: Executive team + PR team

### 9.3 Response Guidelines

**Do:**
- Respond quickly and transparently
- Acknowledge the issue
- Take responsibility when appropriate
- Provide solutions and next steps
- Take conversations offline when needed
- Follow up after resolution

**Don't:**
- Delete negative comments (unless spam/abuse)
- Argue or get defensive
- Make promises you can't keep
- Blame customers or external factors
- Respond emotionally
- Ignore the situation

### 9.4 Crisis Communication Templates

**Template 1: Product Issue**
"We're aware of [issue] and take this very seriously. Our team is investigating and working on a solution. We'll update you within [timeframe]. Thank you for your patience."

**Template 2: Service Outage**
"We're experiencing technical difficulties affecting [service]. Our engineers are working to resolve this as quickly as possible. We'll keep you updated on progress."

**Template 3: Customer Complaint**
"We're sorry to hear about your experience. This doesn't meet our standards. Please DM us your details so we can make this right."
